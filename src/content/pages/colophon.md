## Production

Terra Chartarum is a statically generated site. Every page is built from a registry of scholarly records and passes through an integrity gate before publication. The finished site is distributed across Cloudflare's network. No page is generated on a server when you visit; the only computing is the Atlas filtering its layers in your browser.

## Technology

The site is built with Astro and TypeScript. Maps are rendered with MapLibre; historical imagery is delivered through IIIF and viewed with OpenSeadragon. Typography combines Cinzel and Crimson Pro, with EB Garamond for Greek text and IBM Plex Mono for labels. All fonts are self-hosted.

Every image, dataset and map layer shows its own licence, drawn from its record in the registry. Third-party material retains the rights of its original owners; its inclusion in Terra Chartarum does not imply relicensing or a transfer of rights.

For historical sources, copyright status and permission to reproduce a particular edition or digital surrogate are recorded as separate matters. A source may be out of copyright while a modern reproduction remains subject to distinct rights or conditions.

## The earlier edition

The first edition of Terra Chartarum is preserved, unchanged, at terra-chartarum.pages.dev. Its old addresses that have a counterpart here redirect to it. The earlier edition is retained as a historical record of the project's development.
