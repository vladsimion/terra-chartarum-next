## Production

Terra Chartarum is a static site: every page is generated at build time from a registry of scholarly records and published to a content delivery network. Nothing is computed when you visit.

## Technology

The site is built with Astro and TypeScript. Maps are drawn with MapLibre; historical imagery is served through IIIF and viewed with OpenSeadragon. Type is set in Cinzel, Crimson Pro and IBM Plex Mono, self-hosted.

## Licences

The code is released under the MIT licence. Original prose and original data are released under CC BY 4.0, which means you may reuse them with attribution.

Every image, dataset and layer shows its own licence, taken from its record in the registry. Third-party material keeps its owner's rights and is never relicensed by appearing here. Whether the text of a source is out of copyright and whether its reproduction is cleared are recorded as separate facts.
