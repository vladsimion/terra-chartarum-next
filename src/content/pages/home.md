Terra Chartarum is a digital historical atlas and a publication about the history of maps: who made them, what they set out to show, what they left out, and what the surviving evidence lets us know.

Each essay takes one historical question and argues it from the sources, showing how each conclusion is reached.

The Atlas lets you work with the same evidence: combine historical layers to build, test and question your own reading. Every feature, boundary and line carries its provenance: what it rests on, and how it is known.
