Terra Chartarum treats maps as primary sources. A map has a maker, a market, a projection, things it leaves out and things it wants you to believe; the work here is to say which, and how we know.

The site brings together visual essays, a historical atlas built on shared layers, a catalogue of map objects and their makers, and a bibliography. Every claim points at the evidence behind it, and where the evidence is thin the page says so.
