<h2 id="time-control">The time control</h2>

What the year field and the slider do, and that a feature shows when the year is inside its earliest start to its latest end.

<h2 id="uncertain-dates">Uncertain dates</h2>

What "Date uncertain" means: the feature is only possibly valid at the chosen year, so it is drawn paler and named in the legend.

<h2 id="out-of-range">Out of range</h2>

Why a layer whose period does not include the year stays on the map and is marked "Out of range" instead of being removed.
