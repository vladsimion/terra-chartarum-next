# Editorial review records

Worksheets the editor completed to clear import warnings, kept as the record of who decided what and on what evidence. Each row carries the evidence found in the record, any suggestion made, and the editor's answer; the records themselves hold only the result.

| File | What it settled |
|---|---|
| `map-warnings-2026-10-20.csv` | `witness_kind` and `makers` of the imported maps (FAM-REQUIRED warnings). 26 `witness_kind` values: 8 confirmed by the editor, 18 accepted from suggestions based on the holder named in the record. 10 makers: two linked to new cartographer records (`ptolemy`, `eratosthenes`), eight confirmed unknown from the legacy site's own maker text (`field_status.makers: unknown`, ADR 0011 amendment). The rows left blank (18 `witness_kind`, 14 makers) are not yet verified; their question columns say what is missing. |

A suggestion column is not an answer: only the two `editor_` columns were applied.
