# Bias-audit score sheets

The rubric is in `FRAMEWORK.md` ("Bias audit"). Each sheet scores one unit, a chapter or a section on one period, of one edition from `../edition-register.md`. Sheets are paired by the comparative control rule: the same period, scored by the same rule, in the same week.

| [test-it2003-271-900.md](test-it2003-271-900.md) | IT2003 vol. I, the chapter or chapters on the same period | 271 to the Hungarian conquest | **Not scored**: no full online copy found (the Mureș PDF is an excerpt from 900 on) |

These two are the rubric's test scorings (TCX-77). The period is the one the programme's first question turns on, and the one the four-witness collation also reads. Scoring both will show whether the rubric separates practice from provenance, before it is used on anything else.

The ET1986 draft found that P1 and P2 cannot score a text that cites by section bibliography rather than by claim. The author amended the rubric accordingly and approved it on 24 November 2026 (`FRAMEWORK.md`, "Bias audit").
