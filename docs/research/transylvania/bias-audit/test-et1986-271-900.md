## Notes

What the rubric could not capture, and changes the test suggests:
1. **P1 and P2 assume claim-level citations.** ET1986, like ERT1989, HT1994 and HT2001, uses section bibliographies, so both criteria score 0 or cannot be scored, whatever the chapter's practice. Proposed amendment: count a claim as cited when the section bibliography names a work for that point, checked by following it. Also record, as a separate count, whether the claim names its own evidence in the text.
2. **Engagement can sit in the notes.** P3 should say whether engagement in the bibliographic notes counts. Here it carries the whole engagement.
3. **OCR sampling.** The every-tenth-sentence rule needs a stated sentence split, here a mechanical one, so another reader can draw the same sample.
4. **Context by edition.** Funding differs between editions of the same text (1986: none stated; 1989: a bank; 2001: Soros). C2 is per edition, not per work.

**What the check shows:**
- All seven words are genuine Gothic and attested in the Bible.
- Four of the six in #25 are glossed correctly.
- In #24, *atisk* means a field of standing grain, not a green crop.
- In #25, two glosses are narrowed beyond the evidence. *hairda* becomes cattle, and *wrēþus* becomes sheep, though its only attestation is the herd of swine.
