## 4. Pârvan, *Dacia*: the Romanian edition

| | |
|---|---|
| As cited | Pârvan, Vasile, *Dacia: An Outline of the Early Civilizations of the Carpatho-Danubian Countries* (Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1928). "Verify the Romanian edition." The Stage III section asks whether it is 1937 "…civilizațiile străvechi din regiunile carpato-danubiene" or 1967 (tr. Radu Vulpe) "…civilizațiile antice din țările carpato-danubiene". |
| Verified | **Both exist; they are different editions.** The English edition is confirmed (Cambridge University Press, 1928; x + 216 pp., 16 plates; reissued 2015). The **first Romanian edition** is *Dacia: Civilizațiile străvechi din regiunile carpato-danubiene*, translated by Radu Vulpe from the unpublished French original (Bucharest: Asociația Academică "Vasile Pârvan", 1937). Editura Științifică reprinted it under the same title in 1957 and 1958. The title *Dacia: Civilizațiile antice din țările carpato-danubiene* belongs to the **later revised and annotated edition** (Bucharest: Editura Științifică, 1967, 260 pp.), reprinted in 1972 and 1976. Vulpe is the translator of 1937, not of 1967 only. |
| Corrected citation | Pârvan, Vasile, *Dacia: An Outline of the Early Civilizations of the Carpatho-Danubian Countries* (Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1928). Romanian: *Dacia: Civilizațiile străvechi din regiunile carpato-danubiene*, tr. Radu Vulpe (Bucharest: Asociația Academică "Vasile Pârvan", 1937); revised and annotated edition *Dacia: Civilizațiile antice din țările carpato-danubiene* (Bucharest: Editura Științifică, 1967). RO/W. |
| Not confirmed | That the 1967 edition is the fourth (one bookseller says so); who revised and annotated it. Both from the copy's title page. |
| Consequence for Stage III | The question "who first combined *Carpato-Danubiano-Pontic*, and when?" should be checked against both titles: 1937 says *regiunile carpato-danubiene*, 1967 *țările carpato-danubiene*; neither title is *Pontic*. A 1937 facsimile is online (CIMEC), so the 1937 text can be searched directly. |
| Sources | *The Classical Review* 42/6 (1928) and *Journal of Roman Studies* reviews (Cambridge Core); Cambridge University Press catalogue; RCIN repository; CIMEC digital library (the 1937 facsimile, title page quoted above); Romanian Wikisource author page; enwiki "Dacia (Pârvan)"; bookseller listings (Okazii, Târgul Cărții, Anticariat Arhaic, Galeriile Cișmigiu) for the 1957, 1958, 1967, 1972 and 1976 printings only. |
