# Edition register: the History of Transylvania

Stage II Phase 0 (TCX-67), story TRANS-0-US2 (TCX-75). Research programme: "Transylvania, Wallachia and Moldavia: The Making of Historical Territories" (sections "What the earlier critique taught us", "Bibliography" and "Risks and open questions").

**Rule (programme, design rule from the critique):** every citation names the exact edition, year and page. A citation to "the History of Transylvania" with no edition is not accepted into the evidence register. Cite by the **siglum** in the first column, then volume and page: `ET1986 I, 412`.

**Status: draft, 22 November 2026.** Compiled from library catalogues and repository records, not yet from the books in hand. For each field, **Source** says where it was found. *Unconfirmed* means the catalogues disagree or only one weak source gives it. The column **Copy consulted** stays empty until a person has read that copy; until then, **Copy located** says where one can be read. A row is complete when Copy consulted is filled and nothing in it is unconfirmed.

## C. The four-witness collation (open question)

The programme asks how far the 2001–02 English text of the Roman Dacia and Migration-period chapters differs from 1986. The plan is to collate **ET1986, ERT1989, HT1994 and HT2001** on three passages: the Dacian population; the fate of the provincial population after the evacuation; and Bóna's Migration-period chapter. Any revision is attributed to the edition where it first appears.

A copy of each witness is now located: ET1986 and ERT1989 at REAL-EOD, HT1994 at REAL-EOD and the Internet Archive, and HT2001 at MEK. **The collation can start.** Its first step is to record, for each witness, the volume and page range of the three passages in the Copy consulted column above.
